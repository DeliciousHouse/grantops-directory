# Operating Model

## Recommended adaptation of the shared X playbook

The linked post proposes a bot-operated directory: select a recurring-search niche, build a directory, publish one useful page on a routine, pursue affiliate/referral agreements, and send revenue reports. We are adopting the operating pattern—not its unsupported income claims.

## Truth boundary

**Source-backed pattern:** autonomous research, static directory pages, recurring publishing, affiliate/referral monetization, and scheduled reports are technically feasible.

**Unverified claims from the post:** “$0 on top of the sub,” first revenue by month three, a specific $217 outcome, zero human involvement, and indefinite compounding. These are promotional claims, not forecasts.

## Agent charter

Mogul owns one outcome: build a credible niche directory that produces qualified commercial referrals.

Finished work means:

1. A page is useful to a buyer without requiring an affiliate click.
2. Every material fact has a primary source and checked date.
3. The page passes build, link, schema, and disclosure checks.
4. Publication is logged with its source record.
5. Revenue is reported from verified platform or payment records only.

Mogul may research, draft, code, test, and prepare outreach autonomously. Mogul must not fabricate proof, accept contractual terms, spend money, move funds, make legal/tax attestations, or expose credentials.

## Initial pipeline

1. Capture complaints and purchase questions from current public sources.
2. Score niches for intent, evidence quality, competition, affiliate availability, and risk.
3. Build a structured listing dataset with provenance.
4. Publish a small, high-quality seed set before scaling.
5. Apply to relevant affiliate/referral programs using accurate traffic and audience facts.
6. Publish comparison, alternative, use-case, and geographic pages only when source coverage supports them.
7. Review analytics, search queries, clicks, and verified revenue weekly.
8. Kill or revise the niche if evidence does not improve after the defined evaluation window.

## Reconsideration triggers

Re-evaluate the niche if any of these occur:

- primary-source data cannot be maintained reliably;
- no legitimate affiliate/referral path exists;
- the niche requires professional or regulated advice;
- search results are dominated by authoritative incumbents with no underserved angle;
- pages attract impressions but no qualified clicks;
- partner terms require unsupported rankings or editorial control.
